# A short Galactacians playtest

Use a fresh browser profile on a phone or computer. Allow about ten minutes per player. These are observation prompts, not completed research or evidence of learning effectiveness.

1. Open the first mission and say: "Keep Earth safe. Try anything you like." Let the player find their first placement without pointing to a button.
2. Watch whether they can identify each alien's lane and where a selected tower can attack. Note accidental placements and any confusion between the arrival arrow and a projectile.
3. When armor or a crowd arrives, note their response before offering help. After the wave, ask what their chosen defender did.
4. Let them use math when they want more energy. Note whether the ten-second hint and twenty-second answer feel helpful, whether they notice the return to combat, and whether the three-energy reward is spent on a clear planned defense.
5. If Earth loses, let them retry with the fresh allowance. Ask: "What would you change this time?" Record the change, not just the outcome.
6. Finish with: "What was fun? What was confusing? Would you play another mission?" Avoid telling them the expected answer.

Record device, prior tower-defense experience, chosen math level, time to first placement, misplacements, defenders used, questions attempted, use of help, outcome, and voluntary replay. Do not treat response speed or a win as proof of learning. Observe several players with different confidence levels before tuning around a single session.

The next revision should follow what players actually struggle with: lane recognition, placement, interpreting a counter, energy planning, or the math interruption. Preserve useful choices while fixing the confusing part.
